Privacy Policy

Ore Crusher by MakWhy Studios
Last updated: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ore Crusher ("the Game", "we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the Game.

1. Information We Collect

Ore Crusher does not require you to create an account and does not directly ask you to provide personal information such as your name, home address, or phone number.

However, third-party services integrated into the Game may automatically collect certain information, including:

  • Device information
  • Advertising identifiers
  • IP address
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • App interaction and advertising data
  • Crash and diagnostic information

2. Advertising

Ore Crusher may use Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. AdMob and its advertising partners may collect and use device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, and other information to provide, measure, and improve advertising.

Depending on your region and consent choices, ads may be personalized or non-personalized.

You can learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services .

3. Consent for Advertising

Where required by law, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the Game may request your consent before serving personalized ads or using certain advertising technologies.

You may be able to change your privacy or advertising choices through the consent settings presented in the Game.

4. Local Game Data

Ore Crusher stores gameplay progress locally on your device. This may include:

  • Game progress
  • Player level and XP
  • Virtual currency balance
  • Upgrade levels
  • Unlocked content
  • Audio and vibration settings
  • Offline earnings information

This information is used only to provide the Game's features and restore your progress.

5. In-App Purchases

The Game may offer optional in-app purchases through Google Play. Payment processing is handled by Google Play. We do not directly receive or store your full payment card information.

Google may process purchase information in accordance with its own privacy policies.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers used by the Game, including Google services such as Google Play and Google AdMob, for purposes such as:

  • Advertising
  • Fraud prevention
  • Analytics and measurement
  • App performance and diagnostics
  • Payment processing

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the Game. Third-party services are responsible for protecting data they process under their own terms and privacy policies.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the Game.

Data processed by third-party services may be retained according to the policies of those service providers.

Because Ore Crusher does not currently require user accounts, there is no separate in-game account deletion process.

9. Children's Privacy

Ore Crusher is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If the Game is made available to younger audiences, applicable Google Play and advertising requirements will be followed.

10. Third-Party Services

The Game may use the following third-party services:

  • Google Play
  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play Billing

These services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised.
